Data Analyst — COVID-19 Air Quality Project

Python · Pandas · scikit-learn · Semester 1, 2021

Proposed and informally led a 3-person team investigating whether Victoria's air quality measurably improved during COVID-19 lockdowns. Sourced data directly from EPA Victoria (not publicly downloadable at the time), built a 4-stage pipeline joining four datasets, and used Mutual Information analysis after ruling out linear correlation. Honest finding: no statistically significant correlation between reduced mobility and pollutant levels.
