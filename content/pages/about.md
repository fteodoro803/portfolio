I'm a Melbourne-based full-stack developer. My pattern across projects has been the same one, whether the domain is transport, healthcare queues, or carpooling: find a real gap in an existing system, then build the practical, working version of the fix — not a slide deck about it.

I grew up in the Philippines, where I saw plenty of infrastructure fixes that looked good on paper but didn't actually reach the person who needed them most. That's shaped what I look for in a problem worth solving: does this actually land for the person using it, not just work in theory.

Outside of the projects on this site, I'm a tinkerer — I'd rather understand how a piece works than take it on faith, though I'm deliberately practicing the opposite muscle too: trusting the bigger picture first, especially with AI-assisted tools, and drilling in only where it matters.
