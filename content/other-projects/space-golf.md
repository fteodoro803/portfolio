4-person university team project (COMP30019 Graphics and Interaction). 

Built the game's procedural planet-generation system from scratch: Perlin-noise terrain projected onto sphere meshes across 3 playable levels — plus a custom Unity Editor tool for iterating on terrain, the ball's particle trail system, and session/state management. 

Evaluated positively by 7 outside usability-test participants.
