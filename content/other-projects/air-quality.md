Proposed and led a 3-person team investigating whether Victoria's air quality measurably improved during COVID-19 lockdowns. 

Sourced data directly from EPA Victoria, built a 4-stage pipeline joining four datasets, and used Mutual Information analysis after ruling out linear correlation. 

Findings: no statistically significant correlation between reduced mobility and pollutant levels.
